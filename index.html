How the studio works

Three steps from manuscript to new world.

  1. 01

    Drop & scan

    Drop a PDF, DOCX, or TXT. We extract clean text on your device, then chunk it on paragraph boundaries so the AI never cuts a sentence in half.

  2. 02

    Tune & lock

    Pick a tone — literal, literary, or academic. Lock proper nouns to keep characters and locations consistent across every chapter.

  3. 03

    Read in parallel

    Open the studio and watch your translation cascade in. Sync-scroll the panes to compare every paragraph, side by side.
